Typing Telemetry

Engineered For Real Coding Velocity

Accurate calculations designed around real programming syntax, brackets, punctuation, and symbol handling.

Standard Net WPM

Computed strictly as ((correct_characters / 5) / elapsed_minutes). Starts precisely on your first keystroke and stops at completion.

Strict Syntax Accuracy

Instant visual mistake feedback with wavy error underlines. Accurately tracks curly braces, quotes, semicolons, brackets, and newlines.

Rhythm Progression

Detailed post-challenge breakdown showing interval WPM bars, keystroke consistency, raw vs net speed, and session history stored locally.

Core Philosophy

Why CodeReflex Exists

Our Vision

Direct Thought-to-Code Velocity

Developers think in algorithms, structures, and systems. We believe typing should never be the bottleneck. CodeReflex bridges the gap between conceiving an idea and typing it out smoothly without awkward keyboard finger-twisting on symbols like {}, [], (), =>, or ;.

Our Mission

Real Syntax Over Random Words

Typing "the quick brown fox" does nothing for your programming velocity. CodeReflex grounds practice entirely in authentic programming idioms across HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Python, paired with visual instant gratification through real-time component construction.
